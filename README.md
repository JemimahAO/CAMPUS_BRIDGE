# CampusBridge

**Bridging ambition with opportunity.**

CampusBridge is a student guidance initiative connecting Ugandan students with study opportunities at **Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU)** in Northern Cyprus.

Our goal is to make the process of exploring academic pathways, understanding admission requirements, and preparing for the transition to university clearer and more accessible.

## What We Do

- **Academic Guidance:** Help prospective students explore available programmes and identify suitable academic pathways.
- **Admission Guidance:** Provide information on entry requirements, application procedures, and supporting documentation.
- **Application Assistance:** Offer free initial academic assessments and guidance through EMU's official application process.
- **Travel & Arrival Support:** Optional paid assistance with travel preparation, arrival planning, accommodation information, and settling into the local environment.

## Academic Pathways

We help students explore opportunities across different levels of study, subject to EMU's current programme availability and admission requirements:

- Associate programmes
- Undergraduate programmes
- Diploma holders and transfer pathways
- Master's programmes
- Doctoral programmes
- Research enquiries

## Official University Resources

Applications, admission decisions, programme information, tuition payments, and other university services remain under the authority of Eastern Mediterranean University.

- [EMU – Prospective Students](https://www.emu.edu.tr/prospectivestudents)
- [EMU – Online Application Portal](https://apply.emu.edu.tr/)
- [EMU – Graduate Institute](https://grad.emu.edu.tr/en)
- [EMU – Student Dormitories](https://dormitories.emu.edu.tr/en)
- [EMU – Transportation Services](https://transportation.emu.edu.tr/en/bus-services)

**Important:** Applicants should submit applications and make university payments through EMU's official channels. CampusBridge does not charge an application fee or collect tuition payments.

## Our Position

CampusBridge is an independent student guidance initiative. It does not claim to be an officially appointed EMU recruitment representative or partner. Any formal institutional relationship will be communicated only after written approval from the university.

Admission, scholarships, visas, and accommodation are subject to the relevant university and government authorities. We do not guarantee admission, scholarships, or visa approval.
